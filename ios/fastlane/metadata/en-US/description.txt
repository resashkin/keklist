Your whole life in the palm of your hand. keklist is a private journal and mood diary: every moment is one emoji and a few words, with photos, videos and voice notes alongside.

Private and offline. No account, no cloud, no ads. Everything you write and shoot stays on your phone and never leaves your device.

WHAT YOU CAN DO
• Capture a moment in seconds: pick an emoji, write a line, done
• Add photos, videos, video circles and voice notes
• See your life as a gallery and a calendar
• Track your mood with your own emotions
• Grow your emotional intelligence: build a personal tree of emotions (Joy → Serenity → Calm) and learn what you feel and why
• Look back with reflections; use it as a gratitude journal, reflection journal or daily log
• Open keklist on your computer: your phone works as the server on your local network, protected by a PIN code, and nothing goes to the cloud
• Search every entry; export and import CSV

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
Your notes, photos and videos are stored only on your device. keklist never uploads your journal.
